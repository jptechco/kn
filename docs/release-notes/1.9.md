This release redesigns the Settings window, brings Kinetic Notes back to life on macOS 27, and fixes the Spanish menus and the German Settings window.

## Added

* **Choose the layout in Settings.** *Settings → General* gains a *Layout* choice — Horizontal or Vertical — the same switch as the View menu's ([#48](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/48)).
* **The Updates pane has more information:** check your version, and see when updates were last checked ([#48](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/48)).

## Changed

* **Switches instead of checkboxes.** The options on General, Editing, Notes and Fonts & Colors are now switches beside their titles ([#48](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/48)).
* **A cleaner Appearance setting.** In *Settings → Fonts & Colors*, the Color Scheme menu becomes an *Appearance* picker with the same choices — Automatic, Light or Dark — and a tidier look ([#48](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/48)).
* **New and improved translations for the Settings panel.** Every new label is translated into all seven languages, and existing translations have been improved ([#48](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/48), [#49](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/49)).
* **The side-by-side layout loses its drag square.** The small grip at the foot of the note list's scroll bar, left over from Notational Velocity, is gone, and the scroll bar runs the full height of the list. The divider is still dragged by the line itself ([#46](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/46)).

## Fixed

* **Kinetic Notes opens again on macOS 27.** 1.8 quit as soon as it was launched there, because the note editor was told about Light and Dark Mode before it had its colors ([#47](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/47)).
* **The Spanish menus are fully translated.** *Format*, *Window*, *Bold*, *Italic* and *Strikethrough* now read *Formato*, *Ventana*, *Negrita*, *Cursiva* and *Tachado* ([#49](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/49)).
* **The Settings window fits every language.** In German, *Layout* overlapped *List Text Size* and two panes were hidden behind a `>>` menu. The window now widens to fit every pane's name, and German's date columns read *Geändert am* and *Hinzugefügt am* ([#49](https://github.com/jptechco/kn/pull/49)).
