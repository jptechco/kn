Kinetic Notes 1.9

2 October 2026

This release redesigns the Settings window, brings Kinetic Notes back to life on macOS 27, and fixes the Spanish menus and the German Settings window.

Added

  • Choose the layout in Settings — Horizontal or Vertical, in Settings → General.
  • The Updates pane has more information: check your version, and see when updates were last checked.

Changed

  • Switches instead of checkboxes throughout Settings.
  • A cleaner Appearance setting — the same Automatic, Light and Dark choices, with a tidier look.
  • New and improved translations for the Settings panel, in all seven languages.
  • No more drag square under the note list in the side-by-side layout. Drag the divider line itself.

Fixed

  • Kinetic Notes opens again on macOS 27. 1.8 quit as soon as it was launched there.
  • The Spanish menus are fully translated.
  • The Settings window fits every language, German included.

Contributors

  • @tkambler — removed the leftover drag square (#46).
