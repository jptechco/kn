Kinetic Notes 1.8

16 September 2026

  • Line numbers beside your notes, if you want them — turn them on in Settings → Editing.
  • A word count along the bottom of the window, in the same place. Click it to count characters, lines or paragraphs instead.

  • Tidier dividers. The line between your notes and the note looks the same in both layouts, and each layout remembers where you left it.
  • Fixes to the lines between notes in the side-by-side layout.
